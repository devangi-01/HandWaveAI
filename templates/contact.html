Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about HandWave AI.

How do I get started with HandWave AI?

Getting started with HandWave AI is easy! Simply create an account on our platform, grant camera permissions when prompted, and you'll be ready to use our gesture recognition applications. Check out our Getting Started Guide for detailed instructions.

What hardware requirements are needed?

HandWave AI works on most modern devices with a webcam. For optimal performance, we recommend:

  • A computer with a webcam capable of at least 720p resolution
  • A modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari)
  • Stable internet connection
  • Good lighting conditions for optimal hand detection

Is my data secure when using HandWave AI?

Yes, we take data security very seriously. All video processing happens in real-time on your device, and video data is not stored or sent to external servers except when explicitly using the AI analysis features. When data is transmitted, it's done securely using encryption. Please review our Privacy Policy for more details.

Can I use HandWave AI on mobile devices?

Yes, HandWave AI is designed to work on mobile devices with cameras. However, for the best experience, we recommend using a device with a larger screen, such as a tablet or computer, especially for applications like Math Solver where drawing precision is important.

How accurate is the sign language recognition?

Our sign language recognition system achieves over 90% accuracy for the American Sign Language alphabet in good lighting conditions. The system continues to improve through machine learning as more users interact with it. For best results, ensure good lighting and a clear background when using this feature.
