About HandWave AI

HandWave AI is an innovative project that uses computer vision and machine learning to recognize hand gestures for mathematical problem-solving and sign language recognition.

Project Overview

HandWave AI combines advanced computer vision techniques with machine learning to create an intuitive interface for solving mathematical problems and recognizing sign language through hand gestures. Our technology enables more accessible and interactive ways to engage with digital content.

How It Works

Understanding the technology behind HandWave AI and how it processes hand gestures.

1 Gesture Capture

The system captures hand movements through a camera, processing the video feed in real-time to identify hand positions.

2 Hand Landmark Detection

Using MediaPipe, the system identifies 21 key landmarks on each hand, tracking the position and movement of fingers and joints.

3 Gesture Classification

The AI model analyzes the landmark patterns to classify specific gestures, comparing them against its trained dataset.

4 Action Execution

Once a gesture is recognized, the system performs the corresponding action, whether solving a math problem or translating sign language.

Training Process

Our model was trained on a diverse dataset of over 100,000 hand gesture images across different lighting conditions, hand sizes, and angles. We used transfer learning with a pre-trained convolutional neural network, fine-tuned for our specific gesture recognition tasks.
